🧟 Typing of the READ 📖

The undead are shambling toward the library.
Only your typing — and your languages — can stop them.

How to play: zombies descend from the stacks above — type the word over a zombie's head to destroy it. Your first keystroke picks the target; Tab switches targets. Multi-word phrases need spaces. Every word you finish stirs the horde a little faster. Beat all five levels to save the library; three zombies reaching your desk ends the game. Esc pauses.

Corpora from contabulate.org, Project Gutenberg, SBLGNT & Sefaria.
